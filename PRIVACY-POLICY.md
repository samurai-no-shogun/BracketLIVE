# BracketLive Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

## Operator and scope
This policy describes the BracketLive website and Discord Activity maintained by samurai-no-shogun through https://github.com/samurai-no-shogun/BracketLIVE. Features and external sign-in providers depend on the deployed configuration.

## Information handled
Website tournaments store contender names, descriptions, images, tournament settings, matches, and votes. Guest votes use a pseudonymous browser identifier; signed-in votes use an account identifier. Votes include the selected contender and update time. Optional website profiles store email, display name, sign-in provider, and a provider account identifier where applicable.

The Discord Activity uses Discord account IDs to identify tournament creators and voters. Temporary sessions include display name, channel context, a hashed session token, and expiry. Sign-in uses OAuth tokens, which are not stored in the tournament database. The Activity does not request email, contacts, or chat history. Discord tournament and vote records are separate from website records.

Uploaded images include file metadata and an uploader identifier; Discord uploads also include channel context. Spreadsheet imports are processed on your device; the reviewed roster is submitted when you create a tournament.

Website visit analytics are enabled by default and record pseudonymous identifiers, page, time, referring domain, general device type, campaign names, and supported share actions. These analytics records do not store IP addresses, full user-agent strings, full referring URLs, or organizer tokens. IP addresses may be processed for rate limiting, and hosting providers may maintain separate operational logs. Website visit analytics and advertising scripts do not run inside the Discord Activity.

## Use and disclosure
Information supports sign-in, saved tournaments, vote counting, channel access checks, exports, abuse prevention, and website analytics. Cloudflare D1 stores application records and Cloudflare R2 stores images. Discord handles Discord authorization and platform interactions. If configured, Google handles Google sign-in and Resend receives email addresses to deliver sign-in messages.

Published website tournament content is visible to visitors. Verified Discord channel participants, including later participants, can access the channel's saved tournaments. Creators see aggregate vote results rather than a list of individual choices. Exported PNGs have unguessable download links valid for 24 hours; anyone with a valid link can download the image.

No advertising provider is connected in the reviewed release. Enabling additional providers or materially different uses requires updating these disclosures.

## Device storage and retention
Guest website identification cookies can last up to one year. Account sessions use a separate HttpOnly cookie. Theme, mute, analytics preferences, and personal picks use device storage. Discord Activity sessions expire within eight hours. Local Activity practice tournaments stay in browser memory.

Saved tournaments, votes, profiles, and analytics have no automatic deletion schedule. Report date filters do not delete records. Leaving Discord does not automatically erase votes. Export links expire after 24 hours and scheduled cleanup removes expired export files. Hosting backups or operational logs may follow provider retention practices.

## Choices and requests
You can change or undo your vote while voting is open, sign out, and clear browser storage. Clearing storage does not delete server records and may remove guest organizer access. Website analytics can be disabled on the privacy page; supported Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals also disable future visit analytics. This does not delete existing records.

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, contact the maintainer through https://github.com/samurai-no-shogun/BracketLIVE/issues and request a private contact method. Do not post personal information, credentials, organizer links, or identifiers publicly. Verification of your authority may be needed to protect other users' records. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and legitimate security or recordkeeping needs.

## Security, eligibility, and updates
Access checks and other safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Use the service only if you meet Discord's applicable age requirements and local eligibility rules. Providers may process data outside your country. Updated versions of this policy will be published with a revised date.
